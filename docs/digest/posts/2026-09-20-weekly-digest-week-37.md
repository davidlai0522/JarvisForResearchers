## Papers This Week

### [Corrupt Plans, Clean Traces: Evading Chain-of-Thought Monitoring with Plan Injection](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.15989)
*Plan injection is a novel attack where harmful but benign-sounding reasoning is placed in an LLM actor's context, causing it to adopt and paraphrase the faulty reasoning while evading Chain-of-Thought (CoT) monitors.*
The paper identifies plan injection, where faulty reasoning in context is adopted and paraphrased as the model's own without attribution. This attack achieves a Monitor Evasion Rate of 25-33% and a Steering Rate (APPS) of over 80% on large models like 671B-parameter DeepSeek-R1-0528.

**Why it matters:** Assume that context-provided reasoning, even if benign-sounding, can be adopted and paraphrased by LLMs as their own justification.

### [ScienceBuddy: Recursive-in-Recursive Self-Improvement for Interactive Scientific Agents](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.17523)
*ScienceBuddy is an interactive scientific research workspace that employs a recursive-in-recursive self-improvement paradigm to enable continual learning by coupling harness evolution with model reinforcement learning based on researcher collaboration.*
The system connects researcher dialogue, executable analysis, and a pluggable harness within a persistent workspace. It utilizes a recursive-in-recursive self-improvement paradigm, coupling evaluated harness evolution with rubric-supervised model reinforcement learning.

**Why it matters:** Separate the agent's procedural logic (the harness) from the core model to allow for explicit, evaluable procedural refinement.

### [Objective vs. Search: Decomposing What Makes a Good Tokeniser](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.19145)
*This paper disentangles the effects of tokeniser optimization objectives (compression vs. log-likelihood) and search procedures (bottom-up vs. top-down) by introducing two novel tokenisation algorithms, BottomUpLL and TopDownComp.*
The authors introduce BottomUpLL (likelihood-based) and TopDownComp (compression-based) to separate the influence of search procedure versus optimization objective. The results show that bottom-up tokenisers consistently achieve lower bits-per-byte in most settings.

**Why it matters:** The search procedure (bottom-up vs. top-down) appears to be the dominant factor influencing bits-per-byte (BPB) scores.

### [Workspace Models: Lightweight Robotic Memory via Saliency-Driven Supervision](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20820)
*The Workspace Model proposes using training-time VLM supervision to learn a lightweight, latent memory representation, the workspace token, which allows robotic policies to solve memory-intensive tasks with low deployment-time latency.*
This method introduces workspace models, which use saliency-driven supervision derived from inference compute on strong models during training. The resulting workspace tokens achieve a Success Rate of 91.5% compared to 66.8% for DP Naive VLM.

**Why it matters:** Leverage training-time compute on large models (like VLMs) to generate supervision signals rather than incurring high latency during deployment.

### [Coding Agents with an Obstacle-Aware Harness for Safe Robot Manipulation](https://arxiv.org/abs/2609.20822)
*SAFEHARNESS equips coding agents with two obstacle-aware harnesses—one for route planning and one for contact execution—to enforce safety constraints during robot manipulation tasks.*
SAFEHARNESS treats safety as a first-class objective by using two obstacle-aware harnesses for route planning and contact execution. This approach yields a Task Success Rate of 71.9% and an 87.5% Collision Avoidance Rate on SafeLIBERO.

**Why it matters:** To ensure safety in LLM-driven robotic control, safety constraints must be integrated into the planning decision-making process, not just added as a prompt instruction.

## Trends & Observations
* There is a clear trend toward decoupling procedural logic from core model weights, whether through specialized harnesses ("Coding Agents") or explicit memory encoders ("Workspace Models").
* Adversarial research is moving beyond simple prompt injection to context manipulation, as demonstrated by "Corrupt Plans, Clean Traces."
* Agentic systems are increasingly incorporating feedback loops that go beyond simple reward signals, using external collaboration to derive new evaluation criteria ("ScienceBuddy").
* Memory efficiency in robotics is being addressed by shifting computational load from deployment time to training time via saliency supervision ("Workspace Models").
* Safety in embodied AI requires decomposing tasks and applying specialized, constraint-aware mechanisms to distinct operational phases ("Coding Agents").
